Histogram

A histogram counts the pixels at each gray level, from 0 (black) on the left to 255 (white) on the right. Tall bars on the left mean a dark image. Bars bunched together mean low contrast. Compare the two charts to see what the tool did to the pixel values.

Image numbers

All values are measured on the gray version of the image. Simple contrast is max minus min. Michelson contrast is (max - min) / (max + min). RMS contrast is how far the pixels are spread around the mean.
