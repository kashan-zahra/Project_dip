Histogram

A histogram counts the pixels at each gray level, from 0 (black) on the left to 255 (white) on the right. Tall bars on the left mean a dark image. Bars bunched together mean low contrast. Compare the two charts to see what the tool did to the pixel values.

Image numbers

All values are measured on the gray version of the image. Simple contrast is max minus min. Michelson contrast is (max - min) / (max + min). RMS contrast is how far the pixels are spread around the mean.

How to use Image Lab

Try image-processing tools on any photo and see what changes. Nothing is changed on your device until you save.
1
Open a photo Drop a picture on the big box, or click it to choose one. Tap Choose from gallery or Take a photo.
↓
2
Pick a tool First choose a group (Enhance, Edges, Colour...), then a tool. Each tool has a simple description under its name.
↓
3
Adjust and compare Move the sliders. Before and After are shown side by side. Move the sliders. Drag across the picture to slide between Before and After. Don't like it? Tap Cancel.
↓
↺
Keep & add more Keeps this effect, then go back to step 2 and stack another tool on top.
✓
Save Saves the After picture to your gallery.
  • Made a mistake? Use Undo (the curved arrow, top left), or tap any step in the Steps bar (Original → ...) to go back to it.
  • Lab Data (the chart icon at the top) shows the numbers: a histogram and contrast values for Before and After.
  • Inspect (the magnifier on the picture) lets you see the actual pixel numbers under your mousefinger.
  • Save is the blue button at the top right.
  • Every tool shows a short explanation. Tap Learn more for the theory, the formula and the real Python code, with the tool working live on your photo.
  • After keeping a few effects, use Compare with: Original in the Steps bar to see all changes against the photo you started with.
